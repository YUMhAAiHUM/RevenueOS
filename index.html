Questions

What does RevenueOS do?

Two things. We audit how businesses find and win customers, then put the fixes in place. And we build custom dashboards that help them manage clients, tasks and day-to-day work. Some businesses need one, some need both.

What is a GTM audit?

A structured review of how your business brings in and converts customers: where inquiries come from, how quickly and consistently you reply, how deals move forward and which tools hold it all together. You get a written report of what's working, what isn't and what to fix first.

What does execution include?

Putting the fixes in place. Depending on what the audit finds, that can mean setting up or cleaning your CRM, routing inquiries to the right person, automating follow-ups, building outbound workflows or setting up pipeline reporting.

Do we have to do the audit and execution together?

No. Some businesses take the report and act on it themselves. Others ask us to build the fixes. You decide after you've seen the findings.

What are client dashboards?

Web-based workspaces that bring your clients, tasks, calendar and key business information into one place. Each person sees what's relevant to their role, so owners get the overview and the team gets their work.

Can a dashboard be customised for our company?

Yes, that's the point of it. Fields, stages, views and permissions are shaped around how your business works. We start from your process, not a template.

How does a project begin?

With a conversation. Tell us about your business and what you'd like to improve. If we're a good fit, we'll ask more questions, then send a written proposal with the scope, timeline and price.

How is pricing determined?

By scope. An audit is priced separately from execution, and a simple dashboard costs less than one with several roles and integrations. You'll see the price in writing before you commit to anything.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, if you want it. We can stay on for improvements after the fixes or dashboard go live. What that includes is agreed as part of your proposal.

Have something worth building?

01GTM Audit & Execution

Find where revenue is leaking. Then fix it.

We review how your business finds, answers and wins customers, show you exactly where deals slip away, and then build the fixes.

Most businesses don't need more leads. They need to stop losing the ones they have.

Inquiries arrive through Instagram, WhatsApp, the website and the phone. Some get a reply in minutes, some the next day, some never. Quotes go out and nobody checks back. Nobody can say which channel actually brings in paying clients.

It rarely feels like a crisis. It just quietly caps how much the business can grow, and spending more on marketing only pours more water into the same leaky bucket.

The audit

We follow the path a customer takes, from first hearing about you to paying you, and look at every point where they can fall away. Then we put it in writing, ranked by what's worth fixing first.

  • Lead sources and how they're tracked
  • Response time and ownership
  • Sales process and follow-up
  • CRM, tools and data
  • Website and inquiry flow
  • Reporting and visibility

What a project can include

Audit

A clear picture of where you stand.

  • Written findings report
  • Lead flow map
  • Tool and data review
  • Prioritised fix list

Execution

The fixes, built and running.

  • CRM setup or cleanup
  • Inquiry capture and routing
  • Follow-up automation
  • Outbound workflows

Reporting

So you can see it working.

  • Pipeline view
  • Lead source tracking
  • Weekly summary
  • Handover documentation

Every project is scoped individually. You can take the audit on its own, or ask us to build the fixes too.

How an engagement runs

  1. Conversation

    We talk about how your business sells today and what you'd like to change, and agree on the audit's scope in writing.

  2. Audit

    We review your tools and data, speak to whoever handles inquiries and map how leads move through the business.

  3. Findings

    We walk you through the report and agree on priorities. You can stop here and act on it yourself.

  4. Execution

    If you'd like us to continue, we build the agreed fixes and connect them to the tools you already use.

  5. Review

    A few weeks later, we check the fixes are holding up and adjust anything that isn't.

What we'll need from you

Nothing needs to be tidied up first. We'd rather see how things really are.

  • Access to your CRM, inboxes and tools, view-only is fine for the audit.
  • An hour with whoever handles inquiries, because they know where things slip.
  • A list of the tools you pay for, even the ones nobody uses.
  • A few recent deals you won and lost.
  • One person who can make decisions, so priorities don't stall.

02Client Dashboards

Make the moving parts easier to manage.

Custom dashboards that bring clients, tasks, calendars and essential business information into one clearer workspace.

Most software is built for everyone. That's the problem.

Off-the-shelf tools try to serve every kind of business, so they arrive with features, settings and screens you'll never use. Teams try them for a few weeks, then drift back to spreadsheets and WhatsApp.

A custom dashboard works the other way round. It starts with your clients, your work in progress and the people responsible, and shows only that. There's less to learn and less to ignore.

Different views for different people

An owner needs the whole picture. A manager needs their projects. Someone on the team needs today's work. Switch between the views below to see how one workspace serves all three.

Meridian Interiors workspaceSample data

Interface demonstration. All names, clients and dates are fictional.

What a dashboard can include

Client management

Every client in one place with their stage, owner, history and next step.

Tasks and assignments

Clear owners and due dates, with overdue work surfaced before it becomes a problem.

Calendar and scheduling

Meetings, site visits and deadlines tied to the client and person they belong to.

Performance visibility

The handful of numbers you'd otherwise assemble by hand, kept up to date.

Inquiry capture

New inquiries from your website and other channels land in the workspace with an owner.

Automation and integrations

Reminders and handovers handled for you, and connections to tools that offer a reliable way in.

Permissions and data access

Access is planned at the start, not bolted on later. During design we agree with you who can see and change what, and set up each role to match.

Client contact details, pricing and internal notes stay with the people who need them. If someone leaves, their access can be removed without disrupting anyone else.

How we build it

  1. Discover

    We map how clients, work and information move through your business today, and where things get lost.

  2. Design

    We plan the views for each role, the workflows to automate and the tools to connect, and you approve them before we build.

  3. Build

    We build the workspace, bring in your existing data and train your team on their daily use of it.

  4. Maintain

    We fix issues, make adjustments and add to the system as your business changes, if you'd like us to stay involved.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does a GTM audit actually include?

We map how enquiries arrive, who replies and how fast, how deals move and which tools hold the data. You get a written report ranked by what to fix first, plus a lead-flow map.

Do we have to buy the audit and the build together?

No. Some businesses take the report and act on it themselves. Others ask us to build the fixes. You decide after seeing the findings.

Do you replace our CRM?

Only if it's the problem. Often we clean up and connect what you already pay for. If a spreadsheet is enough, we'll say so.

How is pricing decided?

By scope. You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and price before any work starts.

Who will we work with?

Directly with the people doing the audit and the build. RevenueOS is a small, focused studio by design.
